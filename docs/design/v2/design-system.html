Design system

One set of rules for light, colour, type, shape, lines and motion, and for how each moment of the day changes them. Every screen gets checked against this page. Where it disagrees with an older doc, mockup or the code, this page wins, and spec.md is rewritten to match.

Light levels

Light is the loudest thing in the app, so it comes in five fixed levels. A card takes a level from what it is and how its value sits against your normal. Nothing is lit between levels, and nothing glows only to decorate.

  • One hero, one second. At most one L4 and one L3 glow on a screen. Everything else is L2, L1 or L0.
  • Off your normal is strength, never a new colour. An edge card goes from L1 to L2 and its value and status take the metric colour. The border, the tier and the hue never change.
  • Text sits in the dark. White text keeps at least 4.5:1 against what is behind it. The body colour may reach a luminance of 0.18. The code caps it at 0.09 today, which is why the glow cards look muddy.
  • Locked on every glow: 28 dp corners, the grain, the hairline, the black the core fades to, text top left. Free per card: hue, where the light comes from, how high it reaches.

  • Bright where it is lit. The bottom edge is the light colour at full, as in the mockup. The lit part is the value's share of the card, with a soft step of 22 dp into the dark.
  • Never fully dark. At 0 the card still glows across its bottom 12%.

  • On an edge card (Load, Energy on Today) the light fills from the left to the value and fades out over 40 dp. No progress bar.
02

Colour

Each metric owns one ink colour, used for its values off normal, its icon and its marks. The glow tones are worked out from the ink by one formula, so every card of a metric looks like the same light. Ramps below: ink, then light, body and core.

  • Light is the ink's hue at 90% of its saturation and 56% lightness. Body is 80% saturation, 16% lightness. Core is 70% saturation, 3% lightness.
  • Energy becomes Ember orange #F66C28 on every surface. Gold #FFD166 and a red to green scale
  • Recovery keeps one teal. readiness and readinessDeep
  • Evening violet lights only the evening hero. It is a moment, not a metric.
  • Green and amber are for the strap and sync only, never for a health value.

  • Never two neighbours alike. Tier, edge side and shape make a card's design. Colour alone does not. The later card flips its edge first.
  • Tappable says so. A card that opens something shows a 14 dp caret right after its title, at 50% white. A row that opens something ends with the same caret at 30%, after its value. A card or row that opens nothing shows none. Pressed, both scale to 0.98.
  • (i) lives in the card. A 32 dp round button at 10% white, top right of the title row, the same on every card that has one. It opens the info sheet. No "How it works" panel, no separate card.
  • Number and word. The word after the digits ("bpm", "asleep", "today") sits on their baseline in Body 15 at 72%.
  • Pages under a card (Habit Age, every detail) use the same tiers. DotPanel and DotHeader are gone everywhere.

    Motion

    Motion tells you where something came from, what changed, or that it is alive. Springs never bounce. Nothing moves when idle except the live heart rate.

    Frame

    The header and the tab bar are the same on every tab. Pages opened over a tab share one header of their own.

    • Header: avatar (44 dp) left, the moment or tab name over the date in the middle, the strap chip right. The chip shows words only when they matter: "Charging 64%", "Not synced", "18 min ago".
    • Tab bar: four tabs in a centred pill, the active one tinted with its section colour. A black fade 120 dp tall sits behind it, so cards slide under it softly instead of cutting into its edge. The last card ends 24 dp above the pill.
    • Pages (You, Strap, details, Habit Age, Alarms, Firmware): a 44 dp round back button, the page title in Display 24, then cards in the same tiers. No Material panels or list items.
    • Sheets: the Sheet surface, 28 dp top corners, a 36 by 4 dp handle, the same type roles.
    11

    Unfolded

    On the inner screen of the Fold, and anything 600 dp wide or more, the same rules hold in two columns.

    • Two columns with a 12 dp gap and a 24 dp gutter. Content stops at 960 dp and centres.
    • Type and dot sizes stay the same. Cards do not grow taller, they only get wider.
    • The hero and strips span both columns. A Pair or a Tall with its Smalls stays together in one column.
    • Neighbours include cards side by side, so the no two alike rule checks across columns too.
    • Pages opened over a tab (details, You, Strap) take one centred column, 600 dp at most.

    • One dotted number per card. A card has at most one number in dots, its answer. Times, ranges, totals and second figures are text. A list has one per row. This holds on every card, the sleep hero, the Sleep score detail and Your week included.

    • Stage totals are one 8 dp bar split by stage with 2 dp gaps, and a legend in Secondary with a 8 dp colour mark per stage. Awake under a minute leaves its segment and legend out.

    Up and down

    A change has three parts: which way, how much, and whether it matters. Each part has one carrier, so nothing says the same thing twice and nothing needs an arrow.

    Which way, against your normalWhere the light sits: top above, bottom below (edge cards)Skin temperature lit from the top

    • No arrows, triangles or dotted arrows. An arrow says up but not whether up is good: resting heart rate up is bad news, HRV up is good news.
    • No red and green for change. Colour only marks off your normal, in the metric's own ink, never as a judgement.
    • Always say against what: "vs yesterday", "vs your normal", "vs last week". A bare "+3" never appears.
    • A real minus sign (−), never a hyphen. No change reads "Same as yesterday", never "+0". The build mixes − and - today.
