**Calories, calls from any app and updates that install themselves.** OpenStrap 2.2 counts your active calories all day and in every workout, buzzes for WhatsApp and other app calls, backs itself up and tells you when a new version is out.

## Highlights

- **Active calories, all day.** A new Calories goal (300 by default) sits under Steps in Goals and is sent to the strap as its calorie target. The Activity row shows your active kcal with a small ring filling to the goal.
- **A Calories page of its own.** Day shows each hour split into workouts, walking and everyday moving, and where your calories came from. Week, Month and Year show how often you met the goal and how it compares with the range before.
- **Calories in workouts.** Phone workouts get their own active and total kcal, shown live on the workout screen, in the notification and on the summary. Pick a 300, 500 or 800 kcal target on Ready and the strap buzzes once when you reach it.
- **Buzz for calls from WhatsApp and other apps.** Any app's incoming call now buzzes the strap, not just the phone dialer. Phone alerts adds a Steady or Pulsed call buzz.
- **Quiet hours.** Hold the strap's notification buzz back overnight (22:00 to 07:00 by default, or any hours you pick). Calls still ring unless you turn on Calls too.
- **Adjust a nap's times.** Each detected nap has an Adjust times button that shows the heart rate around it, so you can set when you fell asleep and woke up to the minute.
- **Automatic backup.** Once a day while charging, OpenStrap saves a backup zip with your settings into a folder you choose and keeps the 7 newest. Backup and Health Connect each get a page of their own on the You page.
- **Auto sync to Health Connect.** Turn it on and every strap sync and saved workout goes to Health Connect on its own, now with active calories by the hour and each full day's total.
- **In-app updates.** OpenStrap checks GitHub once a day, tells you when a new version is out and installs it after checking its signature. A dot on your avatar shows an update is waiting. You can turn the check off on the Updates page.
- **Better phone workouts.** A phone workout now runs a session on the strap too, so it gets the strap's own heart rate, training effect and recovery time. The live screen shows every figure at once.

## Changes

- Ending a workout takes a 2-second hold, so a brush or a grab mid-interval can't end it.
- Calmer Today and Activity screens: only cards whose light means something stay lit, and list rows show plain numbers instead of dots.
- The evening summary on Today mentions each figure once.
- Health alerts is now four rows, each with a menu of the strap's values.
- "Found by strap" is now "Auto-detected".
- New icons for sports, sync, Find strap and Restart.
- You and Strap pages open by growing out of their button.
- Recovery time for easy workouts follows the strap's own scale: an easy walk now gives hours, not a day.
- A workout's recovery shows its own time apart from what earlier workouts left, and Time to recover names the workout that actually sets it.

## Fixes

- The strap reconnects and syncs again after the phone's Bluetooth restarts, instead of staying stuck on "Connected. Not synced".
- A sync that loses its link now says so and runs again once the strap is back, instead of waiting up to an hour.
- A notification no longer cuts off the buzz of a ringing call.
- A walk you start on the phone no longer shows up twice when the strap auto-detects it too.
- Phone workouts no longer show a low average heart rate when the strap's own record reads higher.
- Phone workouts' training effect is no longer far below the strap's (a 37-minute walk gave 4.5 where the strap gave 16.3).
- A short walk after a long one no longer shows the long one's recovery time as its own.
- Restoring a backup brings back every missing heart rate and activity minute, even on days the strap had partly sent again.
- A night that ended on the day before the export range is no longer dropped from Health Connect.
- Scale names like "Overreach" no longer break inside the word on narrow screens.

## Upgrading

Installs over 2.1 and keeps all your data and settings. Android 9 or newer. Saved workouts are worked out again once under the new effect and recovery model, so their figures may change a little. To use auto sync to Health Connect or automatic backup, turn them on from the You page.
